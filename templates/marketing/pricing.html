Pricing

Priced by minutes of audio

Not per seat, because the work is the listening — a 30-minute call takes three times the work of a 10-minute one, and your bill should say so. Everyone on your team can use it.

How minutes are counted

What exactly is a minute?

The length of the audio you upload, not how long processing takes. Your month's total is rounded up to the nearest minute once — not per call, which would round a thousand short calls into a thousand extra minutes.

What if a call fails to process?

If transcription succeeded and grading failed, the transcription minutes still count — that step ran and was paid for. Re-analysing the same call doesn't count again. If the upload never made it, nothing counts.

What happens when we go over?

Nothing stops. Additional minutes bill at your plan's rate on your next invoice, and the app shows a running count all month so it isn't a surprise. We'd rather you finish the audit than hit a wall in the middle of one.

Can we cancel?

Any time, from your billing page — it opens Stripe's own portal, so you change or cancel without emailing anyone. You keep access to the end of the period you've paid for, and your reports stay afterwards.

Do you charge for the free trial?

No, and there's no card to enter. When the {{ trial.included_minutes }} minutes run out, uploads pause until you choose a plan. Everything you graded during the trial stays exactly where it is.
