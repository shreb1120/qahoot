How it works

  1. 1

    Write your checklist

    Start from a template for debt settlement, tax relief, solar or insurance — or from nothing. Every requirement is yours to edit, mark optional, or delete. Add the phrases that fail a call outright.

  2. 2

    Upload calls

    One at a time or a batch. Qaboom transcribes with speaker labels, then grades the transcript against your checklist — every requirement answered, with the timestamp and the words that satisfied it.

  3. 3

    Review and coach

    Approve or flag any item the grader got wrong; your decision is what counts. Each agent gets a scorecard showing which requirements they actually miss, so the coaching conversation starts with evidence.

{# ── What's different ── #}

Built for people who get audited

Your checklist, not our rubric

Compliance obligations differ by state, by product, by creditor, and they change. Qaboom grades against the checklist you maintain — edit it today and tomorrow's calls are graded the new way.

Every requirement gets an answer

A requirement the grader couldn't assess is reported as not assessed — never quietly dropped. A report always describes your whole checklist, so a clean call and an incomplete analysis can't look the same.

Evidence you can check

Every covered item carries a timestamp and the quote that satisfied it, next to the audio. You're never asked to take a verdict on trust.

The manager has the last word

Approve or flag any item and the score updates. Coaching scorecards count your decision, not the model's — an item you cleared never counts against an agent.

Questions

How accurate is the grading?

Accurate enough to review, not to rubber-stamp — which is why every verdict shows its evidence and every item can be overridden. Qaboom is built so a manager reviews faster, not so nobody reviews. Grade your own calls during the trial and judge for yourself; that's what the free minutes are for.

What happens to our recordings?

Audio is stored so reports can cite it, and kept until you tell us otherwise — we don't delete evidence a compliance report depends on without being asked. Transcription runs through AssemblyAI and grading through Anthropic's API.

Is this legal advice?

No. The checklist templates are built from widely published obligations — the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, the Credit Repair Organizations Act, state rights of rescission — as a starting point. Your obligations differ and they change. Review any template with whoever owns compliance at your organization, then edit it until it matches what your agents are actually required to say.

What counts as a minute?

The length of the audio you upload, not how long processing takes. Your month's total is rounded up once, not per call. A call that fails to analyse still counts its transcription minutes, because that step ran — re-analysing it doesn't count again.

Can we try it on our own calls?

That's the point of the trial: {{ trial.included_minutes }} minutes, no card. Upload real calls, write your real checklist, and see whether the reports would survive your own review.
