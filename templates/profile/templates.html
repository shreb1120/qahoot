These are starting points, not legal advice

Each template is built from widely-published obligations — the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, the Credit Repair Organizations Act, CMS marketing rules, state rights of rescission. Your own obligations differ, and they change. Review any template with whoever owns compliance at your organization, then edit it until it matches what your agents are actually required to say.

Switching templates never deletes anything: the previous checklist is deactivated and kept, and every report stays attached to the checklist that graded it.
